Looking at this 13th-century Middle Dutch charter, I can make out the following text:

Ic hughe van lamslachte vouere van den moere ende hine bondin
gheve ierden sire arnolde van haesende geronnen te doene te weghe
hen alse die dele letren seghlen dien oft haen lesen die noe ons
ermien heneric van grijsen te alle saken hen vint der liede met goe
den valle te grifen te hedochter daer sa vp gredenen te gelden alsoe
goed als se van te eret hesen eer her hen hert van hueshou te ghelden
te haen coneraer ghu lese sn aenvesselne so loe ic berict van
wu zesen sn die snessen van wt toeruaren dinghe hine se cou dee heder
van dine ven van vael dien dic daer ren helpen in dudt leren
et boven vraets suenen die vp storten dat sel vore ter yn
[...] area cant me ist die wi suten ghelt

[Note: Some portions of the text are difficult to read due to the manuscript's condition and fading, particularly in the lower sections. The transcription represents my best reading of the visible text while following the specified conventions.]
