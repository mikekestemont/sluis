Looking at this 13th-century Middle Dutch charter, I can make out the following text despite some damage and fading:

[...] geuet conent stayen sijn
wden die dien conelschijn
sen gelijcke maken vul
e volde yen callen tegeuorde
o uailleke [...] geuerde
t emedene so huen like
j dyst oue alsmede heme
ten doue so wist helpe maghe
ijs is ued node like helue

[Note: Some portions of the text are difficult to read due to damage and fading of the manuscript, particularly at the beginning and end of some lines. The transcription represents my best reading of the visible text following the specified conventions.]
